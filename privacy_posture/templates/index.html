DK DKCISSP
Privacy Posture Assessment — assess a website's cookie-consent & privacy compliance, fully local.
Scan a new website
Checks third-party trackers, pre-consent cookies, consent-banner/CMP presence (incl. whether "Reject All" actually blocks tracking), privacy-policy disclosures, "Do Not Sell or Share", HTTPS/HSTS, and device fingerprinting — mapped to GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, CIPA, India DPDP, and PDPA.
Only assess domains you own or are authorized to test. Loads the page like an ordinary visitor's browser — never logs in or submits forms. Nothing leaves this machine.
