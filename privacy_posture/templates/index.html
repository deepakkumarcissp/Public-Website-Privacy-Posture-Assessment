Privacy Posture Assessment

— assess a website's cookie-consent & privacy compliance, fully local

Assess a website

Checks: third-party trackers, cookies set before consent, cookie-consent banner / CMP presence (and whether "Reject All" actually blocks tracking — dynamic scan only), privacy-policy disclosures, "Do Not Sell or Share" link, HTTPS/HSTS, and device fingerprinting. Findings are mapped to GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, CIPA, India DPDP, and PDPA (Singapore) with the specific clause and remediation steps. The dynamic scan needs a local Chrome/Chromium install; without one it falls back to a static HTTP check automatically.
Only assess domains you own or are authorized to test. This tool loads the page the same way an ordinary visitor's browser would — it never logs in, submits forms, or does anything beyond that. Nothing is sent to any third party; the scan runs entirely on your own machine against the site you enter.
